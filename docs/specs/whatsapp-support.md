# Rule-based WhatsApp support

Handle rule-based read-only customer questions, bounded self-serve quick-reply menus, and explicit human handoff without paid AI:

- Order status, tracking, no-order, and clarifying replies (`settings.tracking`) match any non-archived Shopify COD order in the merchant workspace whose normalized phone belongs to the sender (`order.phone === from`). Customers do not need a prior COD template send first, while quoted Relay message IDs still narrow multi-order ambiguity. Never look up an order owned by another phone number or invent an arrival date.
- If multiple matching orders exist without disambiguation, ask for the order number (`clarify`, listing up to three recent matching order numbers for that phone) and resolve a follow-up `#1001` reply. If no order matches that phone, reply honestly that no linked order was found (`no_order`) and invite them to reach the team.
- Shipping/delivery timing or policy, return/refund policy (distinct from an active cancellation/return/refund action request), and general policy inquiries (`settings.faqs`) may reply even when no order is attached (`AutomationJob.orderId` nullable), using the merchant's published Shopify `shopPolicies` (`SHIPPING_POLICY`, `REFUND_POLICY`, `PRIVACY_POLICY`, `TERMS_OF_SERVICE`) via `read_legal_policies` and/or saved store policy text (`settings.policy`).
- Interactive quick-reply menu (`SUPPORT_MENU` via `SUPPORT_REPLY:menu`): within Meta's 24-hour customer reply window, when human takeover is inactive and `settings.tracking || settings.faqs` is enabled, casual greetings or unrecognized non-escalation, non-acknowledgment texts queue one interactive WhatsApp button menu (up to 3 buttons, ≤20-character titles: `Order status` when tracking is enabled, `Store policies` when FAQs are enabled, and `Talk to support`). Apply a 12-hour per-phone cooldown (60 seconds on explicit `menu`/`options`/`help` commands) so repeat casual messages or brief acknowledgments (`ok`, `thanks`) do not spam menus.
- Button taps inside `processMetaEvent`: `MENU:STATUS` and `MENU:POLICY` route through the same read-only support queue when the corresponding toggle is active; `MENU:HUMAN` (`Talk to support`) immediately pauses automation (`human = true` across customer threads), marks threads unread for staff review, and queues one polite handoff acknowledgment within the 24-hour window unless staff already replied manually.
- Active cancellation, return, refund action, confirmation text without a signed button, address edit, wrong/damaged item, or explicit human-agent requests do not auto-reply with tracking/policies or menus; they escalate to human handling (`human = true`) and remain in Inbox / Tasks for merchant review.
- Human takeover (manual `Take over`, sending a manual live reply from Inbox, or tapping `Talk to support`) takes precedence and pauses automated replies and menus across the customer's threads until `Resume assistant` is used.

Persist each recognized inbound reply or menu selection alongside its `SUPPORT_REPLY` job atomically. Check the 24-hour inbound customer-service window and existing live outbound or owned-number test authorization both at intake and before sending. Track provider acceptance, delivery, and failures through `Messages` (`kind: 'SUPPORT_REPLY'` or `'SUPPORT_MENU'`) and `Reliability`. Keep unknown send outcomes conservative for review. Test with fixtures and mocked providers.
